Molecular biology can feel abstract — until it isn't.

Cells, DNA, and the central dogma are often taught as lists of vocabulary to memorize rather than ideas that connect to real bioengineering happening right now — CRISPR therapies, mRNA vaccines, engineered proteins. Students end up able to define terms without ever seeing why any of it matters.

A curriculum that builds understanding one lesson at a time.

SynBase breaks the SiBRP Session 2 slide deck into short, focused lessons — each paired with the original figures and a check-your-understanding question before you move on. Modules unlock in order, so foundational ideas from cells and DNA are always in place before you reach engineering applications.

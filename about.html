How It Works

A curriculum that builds understanding one lesson at a time.

SynBase breaks the SiBRP Session 2 slide deck into short, focused lessons — each paired with the original figures and a check-your-understanding question before you move on.

1. Work through a module

Five modules, unlocked in order, walking from the biology of cells through DNA, engineering applications, and gene delivery.

2. Check your understanding

Every lesson ends with a short question, so you find out right away whether an idea actually landed.

3. Try hands-on activities

Work through a real p53 gene sequence and visualize its protein structure, just like students did in the original session.

4. Hear from guest speakers

Sessions close with a talk from a working researcher, connecting the curriculum to science happening today.

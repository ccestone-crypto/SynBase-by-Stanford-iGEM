What is iGEM?

iGEM is an international not-for-profit dedicated to advancing synthetic biology and building the global synbio workforce through education and competition — cultivating an open, collaborative community working toward a healthier, more resilient world. The Stanford team behind this site competes in it every year.

Bigger than a competition

iGEM runs seven programs that support synthetic biology worldwide, from the student competition to policy work to turning research into real startups.

The Grand Jamboree

Every November, iGEM's Grand Jamboree brings 5,000+ participants — researchers, founders, investors, and policymakers — together for the world's biggest synthetic biology event. The 2026 Jamboree runs November 13–16 at the Paris Convention Centre.
